Defect Detective

How to play

  • Look at the picture. Each round shows a real hard-drive photo with a defect in it.
  • Pick the right defect name. Four names are offered. One is correct and three are wrong. You get one try per picture.
  • 8 pictures, 8 defect types. Right answers glow green, wrong ones glow red, and then the next picture appears.
  • Score. Points come from correct answers, wrong answers, finishing the round and your speed. You can give up and keep what you earned.
